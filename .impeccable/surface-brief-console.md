## Scope and visitor mode

**Operate.** The visitor (a reviewer watching a live demo, or the analyst driving it) completes tasks: score an applicant, interrogate the factors, generate and verify a memo, batch-score a portfolio, check drift, edit the SOP, tune settings and retrain. Scanability and state clarity outrank expression; the world lends type, palette, density, and one signature move only.

## Audience, job, action

Hiring managers/recruiters watching the author drive a demo. The job: believe this is a complete, production-thinking credit system. Actions: run a prediction, read the decision, drag what-if sliders, stream a memo, watch claims verified, score a portfolio, trigger drift, retrain.

## Constraints

Every capability in PRODUCT.md preserved verbatim in behavior: predict + decision banner (mockup), SHAP bars, what-if simulator (deterministic), memo streaming + verification badge, batch table + PSI drift states, SOP editor + verifier, settings + retrain status. Fonts bundled locally (offline). No emojis. Disclaimer visible. No fabricated claims.

## Chosen direction — The Bound Logbook

Locked from the direction round (seed key 0119c8ae, assigned card, kind assigned).

### Direction contract

**THESIS:** The console is a credit analyst's bound lab logbook: every consequential action becomes a permanent, stamped, initialed record entry. It refuses the category default of a neutral card-and-gauge dashboard where results appear and vanish — here, everything that happened today is on the page, in order, with initials on it.

**OWN-WORLD:** Graph-ruled paper ground (`#f2efe7`) with hairline blue rules; iron-gall ink (`#1f2a33`); one vermilion (`#bf3f1f`) reserved for stamps, the active decision, and verification marks; graph blue (`#5b7a99`) for rules, tabs, secondary structure, and protective-factor evidence. Type: bundled Archivo (shell/display) + bundled JetBrains Mono (entry face for log lines, readouts, numerals). Component language: ruled entries with timestamp + operator-initials footer (initials set by the user on the Settings folio — never fabricated), stamp marks, page-edge tabs on the rail, entries that append with a press-and-initial animation. Recorded system decision (finish review, fix round): a semantic state trio does real jobs per the Operate mode rules — ok green `#2e6b46` for APPROVE/agree/grounded states, ochre `#8f6408` for manual-review/warning, deep red `#b03a1e` shared with the vermilion family for non-approve/contradicted; risk-increasing evidence is vermilion, protective evidence is graph blue.

**STORY:** The visitor understands in seconds: this is a working record, not a toy — the system remembers everything it did. They believe the numbers because every claim sits in an initialed entry. They do: fill the applicant form, run the model, watch the entry stamp in, drag a what-if slider and see the readout move live, stream a memo and watch its claims get ticked one by one.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Printed header block across the top: product name, today's date, page reference. Left rail: index of sections as colored page-edge tabs (Predict active, Batch, SOP, Settings) above a bound log running the day's entries. Center: the open page — applicant record header with stamp, the verdict line monumental and single (one verdict: grade + probability + decision), then ruled entry rows for each step of the run, then the factor exhibit and the live what-if instrument.

**FORM:** The Bound Logbook — candidate 7 of the grounded list (resonance order: audit file, engraved banknote, Tufte exhibits, bureau disclosure, Swiss grid, broadsheet tables, bound logbook); seed key 0119c8ae.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
